Perc8 · Drum synthesizer

Perc8

An eight-voice drum synthesizer and sequencer for iOS. Perc8 combines pattern editing, chain arrangement, global FX, and master mixing in one AUv3 instrument.

Read the manual Open tutorials Back to apps

What Perc8 Covers

  • Eight independent voices for drums, percussion, and tonal parts.
  • Pattern editing with step triggers, p-locks, fill states, and Euclidean generation.
  • Chain arrangement for building sections, transitions, and full songs.
  • Three global FX blocks plus a master mixer and output routing.

How To Start

  1. Open Perc8 in your host.
  2. Select a voice in the voice row.
  3. Shape the sound in Pattern scope.
  4. Program steps in the grid.
  5. Build a chain when the groove needs structure.
