MIDI Sequencer

Pulseq

AUv3 multi-stage MIDI sequencer for iOS. Supports 1 to 16 stages, with gate behavior, scale quantization, and MIDI CC modulation for elastic, evolving patterns.

Get Support Back to Apps

Quick Start

  1. Open your Audio Unit host.
  2. Add Pulseq as an AUv3 MIDI effect.
  3. Add a synth on another track.
  4. Route MIDI from Pulseq to the synth.
  5. Press play in the host transport.
Tip: If there is no sound, verify MIDI routing first.

What Makes Pulseq Different

Stages
Expandable 1 to 16, each spanning multiple clock pulses
Gate behavior
9 gate modes per stage: single, multi, divisions, probability, programmable, and tied
Modulation
Per-stage MIDI CC lanes for parameter-locking a connected synth
Motion
Forward, Ping-Pong, Random, and Fixed Length play modes
Musicality
17 scales, root selection, swing, gate length, and glide/portamento

Usage Notes

Plugin type
MIDI only (no audio output)
Best hosts
AUM, Drambo, Logic Pro, GarageBand
Clock
Follow host transport, tempo, and clock division
Transport
Runs in sync with the host clock

Common Questions

  • No sound: Pulseq outputs MIDI only. Route it to a synth.
  • Notes feel out of key: Enable Snap Notes to Scale, or check the root and scale selection.
  • Stage repeats or skips: Check the stage's gate mode and programmable gate pattern.
  • Modulation not landing: Confirm the CC lane targets match your synth's parameter, and that the stage value is not set to Don't Send.
